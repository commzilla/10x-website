What we do

10X Trader gives you a live trading account at a regulated partner broker with 10 times your deposit in buying power. Deposit $500 and you trade with $5,000: your $500 plus $4,500 of extra buying power from the partner broker under its account terms. After every closed trade, your buying power resets to 10 times your new balance, so it grows when you win and shrinks when you lose.

There is no challenge, no evaluation and no sign-up fee. You keep 100% of your profit and losses. We earn through the trading costs on each trade (spread and commission), not through fees.

Your deposit is your risk. Your account has a fixed loss limit of 10% of your starting deposit. If losses reach it, the partner broker closes the account automatically. Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. The most you can lose is your deposit.

Why we built it

We're traders too. We watched good traders stay small because their account was small, or spend month after month on challenge fees for funded accounts that were simulated. We wanted a third way: real buying power on a real account, where your results are your own.
