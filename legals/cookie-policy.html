2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website or use an app. We also use related technologies such as pixels, tags, local storage, software development kits (SDKs), and analytics identifiers. In this Policy we refer to all of these as "cookies". Cookies may be set by us (first-party cookies) or by service providers acting on our behalf (third-party cookies). They may last only for a single session (session cookies) or remain on your device for a defined period (persistent cookies).
