3. The 10X Credit Structure

The Broker automatically applies trading credit at a fixed 9:1 ratio on every deposit, so USD 1 of deposit produces USD 9 of credit and USD 10 of notional exposure. Credit is not your money: it cannot be withdrawn or transferred and is cancelled on account closure. You can only ever withdraw your real deposit and realised profits.

The drawdown limit is calculated on your deposit, not on the notional exposure. Because credit increases position size, comparatively small adverse moves in notional terms can consume your entire deposit. When cumulative losses reach the drawdown limit, the Broker closes the account automatically and immediately, without notice. The full mechanics, including per-trade scaling, are explained in the Risk Disclosure Notice.

THE DRAWDOWN LIMIT IS 10% OF YOUR STARTING DEPOSIT — NOT 10% OF THE NOTIONAL. THE FULL DEPOSIT CAN BE LOST DURING NORMAL ACCOUNT OPERATION.
