2. The Credit Structure

The Broker automatically applies trading credit at a fixed 9:1 ratio on every deposit. USD 1 of deposit produces USD 9 of credit and USD 10 of notional. Example: USD 1,000 deposit + USD 9,000 credit = USD 10,000 notional.

Credit is not the user's money. It cannot be withdrawn or transferred and is cancelled on account closure. The user can only ever withdraw real deposit and realised profits.

The drawdown limit is calculated on the deposit, not the notional. Credit amplifies position size — and therefore the dollar impact of both gains and losses — relative to an uncredited account of the same cash size.

10x Standard, 10x Prime, 10x Prime X: minimum deposit USD 500, maximum notional USD 2,000,000. 10x Accelerator parameters are in section 10.

3. Credit Risk — Specific to 10X Trader Products

The risks below are unique to the 10X Trader credit structure and are not covered by standard CFD disclosures. Credit cannot be withdrawn under any circumstances. It is not a bonus, loan, or transferable balance and earns no interest. On closure — voluntary, by drawdown breach, or by the Broker — credit is cancelled and the user has no claim against either party for it.

The real deposit is exposed to market risk from the first trade. It is not held as a buffer.

THE FULL DEPOSIT CAN BE LOST DURING NORMAL ACCOUNT OPERATION IF THE DRAWDOWN LIMIT IS REACHED.
DRAWDOWN IS 10% OF THE STARTING DEPOSIT — NOT 10% OF THE NOTIONAL.

On a USD 1,000 deposit the drawdown limit is USD 100 — 1% of the USD 10,000 notional. A 1% adverse move in notional terms loses the entire deposit. Example: USD 2,000 deposit produces USD 20,000 notional with a USD 200 drawdown — a 1% notional move closes the account.

When cumulative losses reach the drawdown limit, the Broker closes the account automatically and immediately, without notice. Open positions close at prevailing prices, which may include slippage. The Company cannot delay, stop, or reverse closure.

4. Drawdown and Account Closure

The drawdown limit for 10x Standard, 10x Prime, and 10x Prime X is 10% of the starting deposit, fixed at account opening, calculated cumulatively, and not reset by profits. A series of small losses reaches the limit as effectively as a single large one.

Top-ups are not permitted. On closure, the remaining real deposit is returned via the Broker account after positions close and Broker charges are deducted. If the full drawdown has been consumed, the amount returned may be zero.

Users are responsible for monitoring account equity. Dashboard data may not be real-time — monitor directly through the Broker's trading platform. 10x Accelerator drawdown: see section 10.

5. General CFD and Leverage Risk

CFDs are complex leveraged instruments. Do not trade CFDs without understanding them and without the financial capacity to lose the money deposited.

Key general risks

  • leverage amplifies profits and losses; the credit structure amplifies this further;
  • past performance does not predict future results — no strategy, leaderboard, or statistic is a guarantee;
  • markets move rapidly; in fast-moving markets execution may occur at prices materially different from those expected;
  • stop-loss orders do not guarantee a loss level — gaps and low liquidity can cause execution at materially worse prices;
  • spreads, commissions, and financing charges reduce profitability and are disclosed in the relevant Product Schedule;
  • no party connected to 10X Trader — including any Strategy Provider — can guarantee profit.

6. Scaling Mechanic Risk

Scaling is automatic and per-trade. After every trade that closes in realised profit, the Broker recalculates the Account to restore the 10:1 ratio against the user's new equity, by applying additional Credit. There is no minimum profit threshold. Scaling fires once for every profitable close and can occur many times in a single session.

Scaling is not guaranteed. It is applied by the Broker, may be modified or suspended at any time, and is outside the Company's control.

Scaling increases exposure, not real balance. The Deposit and USD Drawdown Limit do not change.

AS AN ACCOUNT SCALES, PROGRESSIVELY SMALLER PERCENTAGE MOVES BECOME SUFFICIENT TO CLOSE IT. UNDER PER-TRADE SCALING THIS EROSION HAPPENS CONTINUOUSLY ACROSS A SESSION — NOT IN DISCRETE EVENTS THE USER CAN PLAN AROUND.

Example: a USD 1,000 deposit that has scaled to USD 50,000 notional still has a USD 100 drawdown limit. A 0.2% adverse move on USD 50,000 equals USD 100 and closes the account. A sequence of small winning trades can grow notional — and shrink the percentage buffer — within a single session without any discrete scaling event to warn the user.

Users must monitor notional exposure relative to the drawdown limit at all times. Dashboard data may lag the Broker's calculation. 10x Accelerator scaling: see Product Schedule D and section 10.

7. Copy Trading and Marketplace Strategy Risk

The Marketplace is a technology layer operated by the Company that lets users browse and connect to third-party Strategy Providers. When connected, the provider's trades are mirrored on the user's account through the Broker. The Company does not execute those trades or control the provider's strategy.

Past performance is historical and not predictive. The Company does not assess provider suitability. Verified / Unverified status reflects compliance and identity checks only — not a performance endorsement or risk rating.

Trades may not execute on the user's account at the same price, time, or size as the provider's. Slippage, latency, liquidity, and account size differences can cause results to differ — and to be worse.

A provider may change strategy, stop trading, become unavailable, or be removed without notice. Disconnecting does not close open positions — they remain the user's responsibility.

Where a provider charges a Performance Fee, it is deducted from profits at the end of each fee period regardless of overall account performance.

THE FULL ALLOCATED CAPITAL CAN BE LOST. PAMM ACCOUNTS ARE SUBJECT TO THE SAME CREDIT AND DRAWDOWN MECHANICS AS INDIVIDUAL ACCOUNTS.

Exiting requires manager approval. The manager may decline or delay based on open positions, market conditions, or accepted Offer parameters. The Company cannot override a manager.

The Money Manager is an independent third party. The Company does not employ, supervise, or control the manager. Verified status reflects a compliance check only. Allocations are pooled and losses applied proportionally — exposure cannot be capped within the pool.

Fees are set and disclosed by the manager and deducted at the end of each Trading Interval. PAMM Investment Accounts are not eligible for bonuses; bonus funds cannot be used in PAMM structures.

For money managers

The Money Manager is responsible for trading decisions and for managing within the Offer parameters disclosed to investors. PAMM management may be a regulated activity in certain jurisdictions and the Manager is responsible for compliance with applicable law. The Company does not provide legal or regulatory advice and may delist a Manager, and the Broker may close the account, for operational, compliance, or risk management reasons.
