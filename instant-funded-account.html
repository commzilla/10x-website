Questions before you open an account

Is 10X a prop firm?

No. A 10X broker account is a first-loss account at a partner broker, not a prop firm account.

Is a 10X broker account real money?

Yes. It's a live account at a partner broker, not a simulated one, and you keep 100% of profit and losses. Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. The most you can lose is your deposit.

Is there a fee to start a 10X broker account?

No. There's no challenge, evaluation or sign-up fee. You pay spread and commission on each trade, and positions held overnight can carry swap charges from the broker.

How fast can I start trading a 10X broker account?

Opening an account typically takes around 15 minutes, and there's no challenge or evaluation to pass first.
