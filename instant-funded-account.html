10X broker account

Instant Funded Account Alternative: No Challenge, No Consistency Rules

An instant funded account lets you start trading without passing a challenge. A 10X broker account has no challenge either, but it isn't a prop firm account: it's a live account at a partner broker, funded by your deposit, with buying power of 10 times your balance.

There's no challenge, no evaluation fee and no consistency rules, and you keep 100% of profit and losses. Opening an account typically takes around 15 minutes. The extra buying power is leverage from the partner broker, under its account terms, and your deposit takes any losses first. Example: a $500 deposit trades with $5,000 of buying power.

Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. The most you can lose is your deposit.

How a 10X broker account works

  1. Open and fund your account. 10X accounts settle in US dollars. If you deposit in another currency, such as pounds, it's converted, and a conversion fee may apply.
  2. Trade with 10 times your balance. Your deposit plus extra buying power from the partner broker, under its account terms. Example: a $500 balance trades with $5,000.
  3. Buying power resets after every closed trade. It moves to 10 times your new balance. Example: a $100 win takes you to a $600 balance and $6,000 of buying power; a $100 loss takes you to $400 and $4,000.
  4. Pay trading costs as you go. You pay spread and commission on each trade, and positions held overnight can carry swap charges from the partner broker.

What is an instant funded account?

An instant funded account lets you trade without passing a challenge first. At prop firms it's usually a simulated account bought with a one-time fee: FTMO's Instant Account, for example, offers $5,000 to $100,000 of simulated capital (FTMO, accessed 26 November 2026). A 10X broker account is different: a live account you fund with your deposit. Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. The most you can lose is your deposit.

Which prop firm has instant funding?

Several prop firms sell instant accounts. FTMO is one: its Instant Account offers $5,000 to $100,000 of simulated capital for a one-time fee, with a 10% maximum loss and a 3% maximum daily loss (FTMO, accessed 26 November 2026). Before you pay any firm, check its fee, loss rules, profit share, and whether the account is simulated. 10X isn't a prop firm: a 10X broker account is a live account at a partner broker. Which instant funding is best?

There's no single best. It depends on what you'd rather risk: a one-time fee on a simulated account, or your own deposit on a live one. Is prop trading legal in the UK?

Check any firm on the FCA's Firm Checker and Warning List before you pay. The FCA has warned that a firm called Funded Trader (funded-trader.net) is not authorised (FCA warning, 12 August 2025).
