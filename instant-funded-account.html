10X broker account

Instant Funded Account Alternative: No Challenge, No Consistency Rules

Skip the challenge fee. Trade a live account with buying power of 10 times your balance.

A 10X broker account isn't a prop firm account. It's a live account at a partner broker, funded by your deposit, with no challenge, no evaluation fee and no consistency rules. You keep 100% of profit and losses. The extra buying power is leverage from the partner broker, and your deposit takes any losses first. Opening an account typically takes around 15 minutes.

Open a 10X broker account

How it works

  1. Open and fund your account. It typically takes around 15 minutes. Accounts settle in US dollars, so a deposit in pounds is converted, and a conversion fee may apply.
  2. Trade with 10 times your balance. Your deposit plus extra buying power from the partner broker, under its account terms.
  3. Buying power resets after every closed trade. Example: on a $500 balance, a $100 win takes you to $600 and $6,000 of buying power; a $100 loss takes you to $400 and $4,000.
  4. Pay trading costs as you go. Spread and commission on each trade, and swap charges from the partner broker on positions held overnight.

Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. The most you can lose is your deposit.

Why trust a 10X broker account

  • It's live, not a simulation. Your profits and losses are real.

Questions before you open an account

What is an instant funded account?

An account you can trade without passing a challenge first. At prop firms it's usually a simulated account bought with a one-time fee: FTMO's Instant Account offers $5,000 to $100,000 of simulated capital (FTMO, accessed 26 November 2026). A 10X broker account is the live alternative, funded by your deposit. Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. The most you can lose is your deposit.

Is 10X a prop firm?

No. A 10X broker account is a first-loss account at a partner broker, not a prop firm account. To see how prop firms work instead, read how prop firms make money.

Is a 10X broker account real money?

Yes. It's a live account at a partner broker, not a simulated one, and you keep 100% of profit and losses. Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. The most you can lose is your deposit.

Is there a fee to start a 10X broker account?

No. There's no challenge, evaluation or sign-up fee. You pay spread and commission on each trade, and positions held overnight can carry swap charges from the broker. 10X earns from trading activity across all accounts (spread and commission), not from fees, and doesn't earn from swap charges.

Does a 10X broker account have consistency rules?

No. No rule limits how much of your profit can come from one day or one trade. FTMO's Instant Account, for example, uses a consistency rule (FTMO, accessed 26 November 2026). Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. The most you can lose is your deposit.

How fast can I start trading a 10X broker account?

Opening an account typically takes around 15 minutes, and there's no challenge or evaluation to pass first.
