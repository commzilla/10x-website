The 10X
model

10X allows you to trade more capital than you deposit. You commit capital as first-loss, and it is expanded into a larger trading account, so you can operate at scale while keeping full control, without raising external capital.

Every time you close a trade, your account rebalances to 10X your new equity. Profits grow your buying power straight away, so future returns are generated on a larger base. Scaling continues up to $2M in exposure, while organic growth can extend well beyond.
